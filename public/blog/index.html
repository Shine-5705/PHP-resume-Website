The Self-Confirmation Gap

Sep 2026
ResearchSelf-Improving AgentsCausal Inference

Coding agents that mine skills from their own logs cannot measure whether those skills helped, because the logs were shaped by the skills. Reported effects overstate identified ones by 4.8× where a skill genuinely helps, and claim a 12% saving where the true effect is ~0. Randomly withholding a skill on 5% of sessions fixes it.

Who Hallucinates Tools, How Often, and What Fixes It?

May 2026
ResearchLLM AgentsTool Use

Commercial frontier models (Anthropic, OpenAI) log zero tool-name fabrications on BFCL multi-turn across 6,000+ calls; open-weight models fabricate at up to 10.4%, and 70% of those are execution-ready state-changing calls. A training-free re-prompt, RVR, removes them — and an ablation shows the fix is driven by the rejection's format, not the registry's content.

Compute-Aware Mixture-of-Agents: Teaching a Multi-Agent Pipeline When to Stop

May 2026
ResearchLLM AgentsTest-Time Compute

A lightweight self-consistency verifier decides, per query, when a Mixture-of-Agents pipeline should stop aggregating. It helps when the agent pool is well-matched to the task (85.0% accuracy, +5 points over fixed-depth MoA-1) and does not rescue a saturated or mismatched pool — an honest look at when adaptive test-time compute pays off.
